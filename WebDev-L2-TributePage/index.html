01 — THE MAN

A Life Built on Dreams & Determination

From a small town in Rameswaram to the highest office in India, Dr. A. P. J. Abdul Kalam inspired millions.

Born on 15 October 1931, Kalam came from a humble family but carried extraordinary dreams.

Known as the “Missile Man of India”, he played an important role in India's space and defence programmes before becoming the 11th President of India.
