01 — THE MAN

A Life Built on Dreams & Determination

From a small town in Rameswaram to the highest office in India, Dr. A. P. J. Abdul Kalam inspired millions.

Born on 15 October 1931 in Rameswaram, Tamil Nadu, A. P. J. Abdul Kalam grew up in a modest family and developed a strong interest in learning and science from an early age. His determination and curiosity helped him pursue his education despite financial difficulties.

Kalam became an aerospace scientist and made important contributions to India's space and defence programmes. He worked with organisations such as ISRO and DRDO and played a significant role in the development of India's launch vehicle and missile programmes.

His scientific achievements earned him the popular title “Missile Man of India”. In 2002, he became the 11th President of India and was widely loved for his simple personality, humility, and close connection with students and young people.

Even after his presidency, Kalam continued to teach, write, and interact with students. He encouraged young people to dream, develop knowledge, and work towards building a better India. His dedication to education and his inspiring vision continue to influence generations.
