A Tribute to

Dr. A. P. J. Abdul Kalam

The Missile Man of India and the People's President

A Life of Inspiration

Dr. A. P. J. Abdul Kalam was an Indian aerospace scientist and the 11th President of India. He played a major role in India's space and missile development programs and inspired millions of young people through his life, work, and words.

His journey from a humble background to becoming one of India's most respected scientists and leaders remains an example of determination, learning, and service.

Journey & Achievements

1931

Born in Rameswaram

Born on 15 October 1931 in Rameswaram, Tamil Nadu.

1960

Joined ISRO

Began working with India's space research efforts and contributed to major launch vehicle programs.

1980

SLV-III Success

Played a key role in the successful launch of the Rohini satellite using the SLV-III.

1998

Pokhran-II

Served as a key scientific adviser during India's Pokhran-II nuclear tests.

2002

President of India

Became the 11th President of India and was widely known as the People's President.

2015

Remembered Forever

Passed away on 27 July 2015 while doing what he loved most — interacting with students.
